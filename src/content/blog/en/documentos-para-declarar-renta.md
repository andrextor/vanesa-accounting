Half the work of an income tax return is gathering the paperwork. If you have it ready before tax season, everything goes faster and you don’t miss any deduction. This is the list I ask my clients for when preparing the 2027 return, which covers tax year 2026.

## Income

- **Income and withholding certificate** (formulario 220), if you are an employee. Your employer must give it to you by March 2027 at the latest.
- **Withholding certificates**, if you are self-employed and were paid fees or for services.
- **Lease agreements** and a record of what you received, if you rent out a property.
- **Pension certificate**, if you are retired.
- **Dividend certificates**, if you own shares.

## Banks and investments

- **Bank certificates** for each account, with the balance on December 31, 2026 and the interest for the year.
- **Nequi, Daviplata and other wallet activity**, which also counts toward the thresholds. I explain how in [do I have to file if I receive transfers through Nequi or Bre-B?](/en/blog/nequi-bre-b-declarar-renta/).
- **Credit card statements**, to know how much you spent.
- **Investment certificates**: CDs (CDT), funds, crypto assets or accounts abroad.
- **Debt certificates**: mortgage, personal loans or car loans, with the balance on December 31.

## Assets

- **Property tax bill** (impuesto predial) for each property, which shows the cadastral appraisal.
- **Deeds or purchase records** for properties and vehicles bought during the year.
- **Invoice or purchase document** for vehicles, or their value in the Ministry of Transport’s table.

## What helps you pay less

These records aren’t mandatory, but each one can lower your tax. I explain how they work in [deductions and exempt income for employees](/en/blog/deducciones-renta-empleados/).

- **Mortgage interest certificate** or housing lease (leasing habitacional).
- **Private health plan certificate** (medicina prepagada) or health insurance.
- **Certificates of voluntary contributions** to pension funds or AFC accounts.
- **Information about your dependents**: children, spouse or parents who depend on you financially.
- **Report of purchases with electronic invoices**, which the DIAN shows on its portal.

## The step almost nobody takes: checking third-party reports

Banks, companies and notaries report to the DIAN what they know about you: how much they paid you, how much you deposited, what you bought (información exógena). You can check that report on the DIAN portal with your user account. Review it before filing: if your return doesn’t match what third parties reported, the DIAN may ask you for explanations. And if you find a mistake, ask the third party to correct it.
