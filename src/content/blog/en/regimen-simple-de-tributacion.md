The **Simple Tax Regime (Régimen Simple de Tributación, RST)** was created so small businesses could pay their taxes more easily. For many it’s a good option; for others it ends up costing more than the ordinary regime. Here is how to tell which case is yours.

## What it is

It’s a tax that **replaces income tax** and, in a single payment, **includes ICA** (the municipal industry and commerce tax). It’s paid through bimonthly advance payments and an annual return. The rate is applied to **gross income**, depending on the business activity and its income level.

## Who can join

- **Individuals** who run a business or work independently in a profession.
- **Companies** whose partners are individuals.

In both cases, gross income for the previous year must be **below 100,000 UVT**. To apply in 2027, 2026 income is what counts: about COP 5,237 million with the 2026 UVT.

Some activities can’t join, such as financial advisory or factoring. It also doesn’t apply to someone who, in practice, has an employment relationship with their client.

## Advantages

- **No withholding tax** on your sales or services, so you receive the full payment.
- **A single tax** instead of separate income tax and ICA, with less paperwork.
- **Credits:** the pension contributions you pay as an employer and part of the income you receive through card or electronic payments can be credited against the tax.

One important obligation: under the Simple Regime you must issue **electronic invoices**.

## When it doesn’t pay off

Because the tax is calculated on **income** and not on profit, the Simple Regime can be expensive if your business has **high costs and low margins**. For example, in a shop that sells a lot but earns little per product, paying a percentage of everything it sells may exceed what it would pay in income tax.

On the other hand, it usually suits independent professionals and low-cost service businesses, and businesses that currently have a lot of tax withheld.

## What’s the deadline to switch?

If you already have a RUT, you can join the Simple Regime until the **last business day of February** of the year you want it to apply: for 2027, the last business day of February 2027. If you are starting your business, you can choose it when you register in the RUT. That’s why November and December are a good time to run the numbers.
